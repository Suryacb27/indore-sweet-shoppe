A Taste of Tradition

Our Premium Kaju Katli is more than just a sweet; it's a piece of Indore's rich culinary heritage. Each diamond-shaped piece is meticulously crafted using only premium grade cashews, carefully selected and processed to achieve that legendary melt-in-the-mouth texture.

Following a secret family recipe passed down through generations, we ensure that the sweetness is perfectly balanced, never overpowering the rich, buttery flavor of the cashews. Adorned with delicate edible silver leaf (vark), it is the quintessential gift for celebrations or a luxurious treat for yourself.

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
